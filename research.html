Research Projects

Extreme Hazard Resilience Assessment of Transmission Tower Clusters

#Resilience #Transmission_Towers #Extreme_Hazards

This research aims to develop a comprehensive framework for assessing the resilience of electrical transmission tower-line systems (ETTLS) under extreme hazards in the Hong Kong region. By integrating probabilistic hazard models with refined structural analysis, we focus on identifying critical nodes within tower clusters to ensure power grid stability during typhoons or other extreme events.

Vision-based Evaluation of Vortex-Induced Vibration Comfort

#Computer_Vision #Vibration_Control #Bridge_Engineering

Utilizing high-speed cameras and computer vision algorithms, this project explored a non-contact method to evaluate the serviceability and comfort of long-span bridges under vortex-induced vibration (VIV). The research provides a cost-effective alternative to traditional accelerometer-based monitoring systems.

Optimization of Design Wind Loads for Suspension Bridges

#Wind_Engineering #Structural_Optimization

Developed an optimized approach for calculating design wind loads on long-span suspension bridges, taking into account the non-linear aerodynamic effects and bluff body cross-sections.
